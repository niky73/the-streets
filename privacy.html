Privacy Policy

The Streets · A Lones Studios project
Effective October 27, 2026

The Streets is in a prelaunch testing phase. Paid subscriptions are not available yet. This policy describes the website as currently implemented and will be updated before paid memberships launch.

Information we collect

If you register, we collect your email address, requested public username, and account authentication information. Authentication is provided through Supabase. We do not intentionally display your email address to other members. Your public username, published posts, comments, and reactions may be visible to other visitors. We also store your selected hometown as part of the discussions you publish, as well as the dates of activity.

We receive reports of potentially harmful content, including the reporting account, the content being reported, the reason, and any details submitted. Hometown requests contain the city and state you enter. Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser data, and access logs to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts, show hometown conversations, maintain community features, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and protect the service. We may use aggregate activity information to understand how the site is used. We do not currently offer paid checkout; if billing launches, we will update this policy to explain payment processing.

Public content and privacy

The Streets is a public discussion website. Posts, comments, reactions, and usernames may be visible to anyone who visits. Do not publish sensitive information about yourself or others. Removing your own post or comment hides it from public view, but it may remain in our records for moderation, security, or legal reasons. Copies, screenshots, or content shared by others may remain outside our control.

Where information is handled

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Accounts and community records are handled through Supabase. These providers process information needed to deliver the service under their own applicable policies and agreements.

Browser storage and cookies

The website uses browser session storage to keep you signed in during your browser session. Authentication and infrastructure providers may also use cookies or similar technologies needed for their services. We have not enabled a separate advertising tracking system on this site at this stage.

Sharing and disclosure

Public community activity is shared with visitors as described above. We share information with service providers as necessary to run the website. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or the service. We do not currently sell personal information to advertisers.

Security and retention

We use account authentication and database access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. We keep account and community information while needed for operation, safety, dispute handling, and applicable obligations. We have not yet finalized a comprehensive deletion and retention schedule.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to register and still read public discussions. You can sign out and hide your own posts and comments using the controls provided. For account-data access, correction, or deletion requests, please contact the site operator through an official Lones Studios contact channel. We are preparing a dedicated privacy contact method before broad public launch; until it is published, please do not submit sensitive personal information through public posts or reports.

Children

The Streets is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Age verification and additional account safeguards are still being prepared before public launch.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the service develops. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Material changes to data handling, including payments or advertising analytics, will be described before those features launch.
