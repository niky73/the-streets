Community Guidelines · Keep The Streets Talking

Bring your opinions, humor, hometown stories, and respectful disagreements. Keep it about experiences and ideas—not attacks on people.

  • No threats, harassment, hate, bullying, or targeted pile-ons.
  • No private information. Do not post anyone's address, phone number, workplace details, private messages, or identifying information without permission.
  • No unverified accusations about identifiable people. Avoid naming or identifying people in allegations of wrongdoing, cheating, abuse, or crime. Use fictional or anonymized scenarios instead.
  • No sexual content involving minors, exploitation, impersonation, scams, or spam.
  • Respect privacy and consent. Do not upload other people's images or personal details without their permission.

Posts and comments may be reported, reviewed, or hidden for violations. Repeated or serious abuse may result in account restrictions. Reports are reviewed by the site owner; moderation is not 24/7. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Please avoid posting sensitive personal information about yourself, too.
